RETRO WEATHER v1.2 - SCREENSAVER
Choose the Windows Screensaver component to install/update Windows\RetroWeather.scr.
Windows selection, timeout and sign-in settings remain unchanged on upgrade.
Player and screensaver share weather/theme and CRT profile preferences.
Close the normal player before testing the actual screensaver. Every saver
session starts with audio disabled; M enables/disables it without exiting.
Disabling closes audio devices and releases PCM buffers. Holding M toggles once.
Other keys or normal mouse movement exit. Preview/basic backgrounds stay silent.
Tools > Screensaver contains app controls and Windows screensaver settings.
The installer requests graceful closure with Retry/Cancel and no forced kill.
