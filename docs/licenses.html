Privacy

This static website uses no analytics, advertising, third-party fonts, cookies or weather requests. Its scene illustrations are generated locally in your browser. The hosting provider can receive normal page-request information, including an IP address. GitHub manages repository, issue and release-download requests under its own policies.

The Windows app sends ZIP/location and forecast requests directly to the providers, who receive your IP and request parameters. Preferences, saved locations and weather cache stay on your machine. Reports and videos are exported locally; nothing is automatically uploaded. Review files before sharing, especially footage of real weather pages.
