Retro Weather selects forecast fields, rounds/converts units, abbreviates
condition descriptions, and calculates summaries for display. These displays
are adaptations of model data, not station observations or an alert service.
Live-data pages retain the provider URL and license credit in their footer.
Help > Open-Meteo Attribution Website opens the provider in the default browser.
Full provider and license links are supplied here and on About.
Benchmark scenarios are locally simulated, not provider observations.

PRIVACY
Live ZIP/location and weather requests go directly from your machine to the
providers. Providers receive the request parameters and IP address. There is
no Retro Weather account, analytics server or automatic benchmark upload.
Preferences, saved locations and cached weather are stored locally. Review
any report/video before choosing to share it, especially real location views.
